# KuralSaga Privacy Policy

**Last updated: 3 October 2026**

KuralSaga is a Tamil-English educational adventure game operated by **Ultra Oorugai Studios**.

This Privacy Policy explains what information KuralSaga collects, how that information is used, and the third-party services used by the app.

## 1. Information We Collect

KuralSaga does not currently require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or postal address in order to use the app.

KuralSaga uses **Google Analytics for Firebase** to understand how users interact with the app.

Firebase Analytics may collect information such as:

- app usage information;
- device and app information;
- app installation identifiers;
- interaction and engagement information;
- gameplay events such as unit starts, unit completions, answers, progression, rewards, and other in-app interactions.

KuralSaga does not use this analytics information to identify users by name.

## 2. How We Use Information

Analytics information may be used to:

- understand how players use KuralSaga;
- improve gameplay and learning activities;
- improve progression and reward systems;
- identify bugs and usability problems;
- understand which lessons and features are being used;
- measure engagement and app performance;
- improve reliability and the overall user experience.

We aim to collect only information that is useful for operating and improving the app.

## 3. Firebase and Google Services

KuralSaga uses services provided by Google, including **Google Analytics for Firebase**.

These services may process app usage, device, installation, and interaction information according to Google's own privacy and data-processing practices.

More information about Firebase privacy and data processing is available from Google:

- [Firebase Privacy and Security](https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy)
- [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)

## 4. Advertising

KuralSaga does not currently display third-party advertisements.

If advertising is introduced in a future version of KuralSaga, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain how advertising-related data is handled.

## 5. Accounts and Sign-In

KuralSaga does not currently require users to create an account or sign in.

If account creation, Google Sign-In, Apple Sign-In, or another authentication method is introduced in a future version, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain:

- what account information is collected;
- why that information is collected;
- how it is used;
- how it is stored;
- and how users can request deletion of their account or associated data.


## 6. Data Sharing

Ultra Oorugai Studios does not sell users' personal information.

Information may be processed by third-party service providers that are used to operate, analyze, and improve KuralSaga, including Google Firebase.

These providers process information according to their own privacy policies and applicable agreements.

## 7. Data Retention

Analytics information is retained according to the retention settings and policies of the services used by KuralSaga.

We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary for purposes such as:

- analytics;
- product improvement;
- troubleshooting;
- security;
- and operation of the app.

## 8. Children's Privacy

KuralSaga is designed for a general audience and is not specifically directed at young children.

The app does not currently require users to provide their name, email address, phone number, or other direct personal information in order to play.

Parents or guardians who have questions about KuralSaga's privacy practices may contact Ultra Oorugai Studios using the contact information below.

## 9. Security

We take reasonable measures to limit data collection and use information only for operating, analyzing, maintaining, and improving KuralSaga.

Data handled by third-party services such as Firebase is transmitted and processed using the security mechanisms provided by those services.

No method of electronic transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure, but we aim to use established and reputable service providers.

## 10. International Data Processing

Some third-party services used by KuralSaga may process information on servers located outside the user's country of residence.

Where applicable, such processing is handled according to the privacy and data-protection practices of the relevant service provider.

## 11. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as KuralSaga evolves.

For example, the policy may be updated if KuralSaga introduces:

- user accounts;
- sign-in functionality;
- cloud synchronization;
- social features;
- advertising;
- subscriptions;
- in-app purchases;
- additional analytics;
- or other features that affect how information is collected or used.

When this Privacy Policy is updated, the **Last updated** date at the top of this page will also be updated.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically.
